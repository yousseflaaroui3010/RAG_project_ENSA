# G1 triage before v1.0.1 (2026-09-12)

v1.0.0's release run passed G1 at the minimum: **36 of 40** fully grounded
answers, target 36. The four misses were `g-in-014`, `g-in-026`, `g-in-033`
and `g-in-040`, all refusals (the product declined; it never invented).
This note records why, so the next run is read against a known cause and not
a guess. No threshold, prompt or retrieval setting was changed because of it.

## Method

1. **Free, local:** the product's own hybrid search (`agent.retrieval`) on the
   real evaluation index, with each question as the query. Is the cited
   passage in the top five?
2. **One focused paid trace (19 provider calls):** each question through the
   real graph with the query rewrite, relevance grader, reword and writer
   ports wrapped to log their inputs and decisions.

## Findings

| Row | Target found by search? | What the trace showed | Classification |
|---|---|---|---|
| g-in-014 | Article 35 not in the first top five; Articles 31-34 and a guide section were | grader rejected two rounds, accepted the third after rewording; answered from Article 35 | **model variation** (answered in this trace) |
| g-in-026 | yes, Articles 43-46, rank 1 | grader accepted round one; answered correctly | **model variation** |
| g-in-033 | yes, Article 66, rank 1 | grader rejected all three rounds; rewording drifted to French-France terms (`CSE`) | **real, repeatable** |
| g-in-040 | yes, rank 1 and 3 | grader accepted round one; answered correctly | **model variation** |

## The one real cause: g-in-033

The relevance grader judges **child chunks of about 500 characters**, while
the writer later reads the full parent sections. Article 66's decisive
sentence -- the employer informs the employees' delegates **at least one
month** before an economic dismissal -- sits just past the start of the
article, outside the chunk the grader is shown. The grader correctly answers
"these words do not contain the delay", and the question is refused three
rounds in a row. The rewording step then reaches for French-France vocabulary
(`CSE`), which the Moroccan code does not use, so later rounds cannot recover.

## Decision (DECISIONS 2026-09-12)

Not changed before the defense. Grading on full parent sections would change
every question's behaviour and put G2's strict 20 of 20 at risk days before a
jury, for one row. The fix is recorded for V1.1: grade on the parent section
(or on the chunk plus its neighbour), and keep the rewording step inside the
documents' own legal vocabulary; then re-run all 60 questions.
